Once upon a time, a tiny ant was wandering near a river. Thirsty, she leaned over to take a drink, but her tiny feet slipped and she fell into the rushing water. Struggling to stay afloat, she realized she was in deep trouble.

A kind dove was resting in a nearby tree and saw the ant in distress. Quickly, she plucked a leaf and dropped it into the water right next to the ant. The ant climbed onto the leaf and safely floated to the riverbank.

A few days later, the same ant saw a hunter aiming his arrow directly at the dove in the tree. Remembering the dove's kindness, the ant rushed forward and bit the hunter hard on the ankle.

The hunter yelped in pain, causing him to miss his shot. The noise alerted the dove, who immediately took flight and escaped to safety. The tiny ant proved that no act of kindness is ever wasted, and even the smallest creature can make a huge difference.
