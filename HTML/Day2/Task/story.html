The dog and the Bone

A dog found a big, juicy bone. He held it tightly in his mouth. He walked home.On his way, he crossed a bridge over a clear stream. He looked down into the water. He saw his own reflection. He thought it was another dog with a much bigger bone.The dog wanted that bone, too. He opened his mouth to bark. His own bone fell into the water. It was gone. The greedy dog lost everything. Moral: Do not be greedy. Be happy with what you have.

The Secret of the Echoing Cave

Liam lived in a quiet valley surrounded by high, blue mountains. He loved exploring, but he always avoided the Black Crag. The older villagers said a strange noise came from its deep cave. They called it the Echoing Cave and warned children to stay away.One afternoon, Liam’s playful dog, Rusty, chased a rabbit straight toward the forbidden mountain. Liam yelled for Rusty to stop, but the dog kept running. Within minutes, Rusty vanished into the dark mouth of the Echoing Cave.Liam stood at the entrance, his heart pounding. The cave looked like a giant monster with its mouth wide open. From inside, he heard a low, rumbling sound. It did not sound like an echo. It sounded like something breathing."Rusty!" Liam called out, his voice trembling.Only a strange, whistling wind answered him. Liam took a deep breath. He could not leave his best friend behind. He grabbed a thick stick, lit the tip with his small pocket lighter, and stepped into the darkness.The air inside was cold and damp. The light from his stick cast long, dancing shadows on the stone walls. As he walked deeper, the rumbling sound grew louder. Liam’s legs felt like jelly, but he kept moving forward, calling Rusty’s name in a whisper.Suddenly, the narrow path opened into a massive cavern. Crystals on the ceiling caught the light of his stick, shining like thousands of stars. In the center of the room sat Rusty. He was not barking or hiding. He was happily wagging his tail in front of a massive, ancient stone statue of a dragon.The rumbling sound was not a monster. It was the wind blowing through a hidden hole in the cavern ceiling, vibrating against the hollow stone statue. The cave was not haunted; it was just a musical instrument played by nature.Liam smiled and hugged Rusty. He realized that fear often makes things look much scarier than they actually are. He looked up at the beautiful, shining crystals and felt glad he found the courage to step inside.
