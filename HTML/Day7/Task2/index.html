Vellore had the previlege of being the seat of the Pallava, Chola, Nayak, Maratha, Arcot Nawabs and Bijapur Sultan Kingdoms. It was described as the best and strongest fortress in the Carnatic War in the 17th Century. It was witnessed the massacre of European soldier during the mutiny of 1806. Vellore district lies between 12° 15’ to 13° 15’ North latitudes and 78° 20’ to 79° 50’ East longitudes in Tamilnadu State. The geographical area of this district is 1336.42 Sq.Kms. The total population as per 2011 Census is 39,36,331.

History and Heritage

Vellore, known as the "Fort City", is a historically significant and industrially vibrant district in northeastern Tamil Nadu. Located on the Palar River, it is approximately 135 km from Chennai. It is a major global hub for medical tourism, higher education, and leather exports

Vellore has been ruled by the Pallavas, Cholas, Vijayanagar, and the Carnatic Nawabs.

Vellore Fort:A 16th-century architectural marvel that houses the famous Jalakandeswarar Temple. It was the site of the 1806 Vellore Revolt, the earliest uprising against British rule.

Sripuram Golden Temple: A massive spiritual complex coated with pure gold.

Vellore is the Head-Quarters of Vellore District which is well connected by Rail and bus routes to major towns of the neighbouring states like Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka and Kerala. The history of the District assumes a great significance and relevance, as we unfold the glorious past. The Monuments found in the district give a vivid picture of the town through the ages. In the 18th Century Vellore District was the scene of some of the decisive battles fought in Ambur 1749 A.D., Arcot 1751 A.D. and Vandavasi 1768 A.D. as a result of the long – drawn struggle between the English and the French for Supremacy.

One of the monuments of Vellore is the Fort. A very close examination of the stone inscriptions suggests that the Fort in all probability might have been built during the rule of Chinna Bommi Nayak (1526 to 1595 A.D.). The Fort is one of the most perfect speciments of Military architecture in South India. The Jalakandeswarar Temple inside the Fort is a very fine example of Vijayanagar architecture. The Kalyanamantap on the left of the entrance, with intricate carvings and delicacy of execution bears testimony to the engineering marvel and advanced state of sculpture of the times. Another landmark that has put Vellore on the Centre stage of Medical world is the Christian Medical College & Hospital.

The outstanding performance of this district in contributing to the Military service is commendable, as more and more men have enlisted themselves to the Military service, to serve the national indomitable spirit and courage. The clock tower in the long bazaar, Vellore was built in 1928 A.D.. A stone inscription in the building reads “VELLORE – FROM THIS VILLAGE 277 MEN WENT TO THE GREAT WAR 1914-18, OF THEM 14 GAVE UP THEIR LIVES” .

Education

Vellore district is a premier educational hub in Tamil Nadu, renowned for world-class medical and engineering institutions. Administered by the District Education Department, the district boasts a high literacy rate with 94.1% of schools meeting basic infrastructure and facility standards. It offers diverse options ranging from elite universities to reputed public schools.

Higher and Professional Education

Vellore is home to globally recognized universities and professional colleges, drawing students from across the country.

Medicine

The district features the internationally acclaimed Christian Medical College (CMC) and the Government Vellore Medical College.

Engineering & Technology

Vellore Institute of Technology (VIT) is among the top-ranked private engineering institutions in India. Additionally, the Thanthai Periyar Government Institute of Technology provides high-quality, affordable technical education

General Arts & Science

Auxilium College and Voorhees College are highly regarded, autonomous institutions offering undergraduate and postgraduate

School Education & Administration

The local school ecosystem is structured to ensure widespread accessibility, operating numerous government, aided, and private institutions

Administration

The district's school system is overseen by the Chief Educational Officer (CEO) and Block Education Officers (BEO), who manage scheme implementation and teacher deployment.

Private and International School

For parents seeking alternate curricula, the district has several CBSE-affiliated institutions like Narayana E-Techno School and Mount Litera Zee School in the Katpadi and nearby areas

Health Care

Vellore is a premier medical hub in India, anchored globally by the Christian Medical College (CMC) Vellore. It provides a robust three-tier healthcare system ranging from grassroots primary care facilities to quaternary teaching hospitals, attracting patients nationwide

Key Pillars of Vellore Healthcare

Tertiary & Quaternary Care

The city is dominated by the sprawling 3,700-bed CMC Vellore, which attracts thousands of patients daily for advanced treatments, transplants, and complex surgeries

Multispecialty & Super Specialty Centers

Institutions like Sri Narayani Hospital and Research Centre (SNHRC) and Sri Ragavendra Health Care provide advanced care in cardiology, oncology, neurology, and gastroenterology.

Public & Government Infrastructure

The district features the Government Vellore Medical College & Hospital (GVMCH) in Adukkamparai, backed by a network of over 100 Primary Health Centres (PHCs) and Health Sub-Centres providing 24/7 basic and emergency services.

Community Outreach

CMC's College of Nursing Community Health (CONCH) drives extensive rural outreach and home-based primary care for nearby villages and urban peripheries.

Economy

  • Leather and Manufacturing: Vellore accounts for over a third of India's total leather exports. The region features extensive tanneries and shoe production clusters in towns like Ambur, Vaniyambadi, and Ranipet.
  • Healthcare & Education: Institutions like the Christian Medical College & Hospital (CMCH) and the Vellore Institute of Technology (VIT) anchor the local service economy. The daily influx of thousands of patients, students, and visitors sustains a large network of local hospitality, transportation, and retail businesses.
  • Industrial Parks: The region leverages major industrial hubs managed by the Tamil Nadu Small Industries Development Corporation (SIDCO) and State Industries Promotion Corporation of Tamil Nadu (SIPCOT). These parks host automotive components, engineering, and explosive manufacturing facilities.
  • Textiles and Handlooms: The district—particularly in the Gudiyattam block—supports a thriving handloom and powerloom industry, manufacturing lungies, synthetic fibers, and apparel.
