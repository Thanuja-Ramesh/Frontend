Company Name:Google

About Company

Google is a leading American multinational technology corporation specializing in internet-related services, artifical intelligence and software. Founded on September 4, 1998, by Larry Page and Sergey Brin, it operates as a subsidiary of Alphabet Inc.

Services

Google services encompass a massive ecosystem of digital tools used by billions of people daily for communication, productivitiy, entertainment and business. The company categorises these into consumer services, hardware, enterprise tools and developer platforms.
