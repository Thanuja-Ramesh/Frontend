The Honest Woodcutter

Main Content

A poor woodcutter lived in a small village and earned his living by cutting firewood. One day, while working near a river, his axe slipped from his hands and fell into the water. He became very sad because he could not afford to buy a new one. A wise spirit appeared and showed him a golden axe, asking if it was his. The woodcutter honestly replied that it was not. The spirit then showed him a silver axe, but he again said it was not his.

Finally, the spirit brought out his old iron axe. The woodcutter happily said it was his. Pleased with his honesty, the spirit rewarded him by giving him all three axes. The woodcutter thanked the spirit and returned home happily. His honesty brought him a reward that he never expected.

Moral

Honesty is the best policy
