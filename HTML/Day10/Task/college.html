Admission Process

The admission process begins with submitting the online application form. Applicants must provide the required documents, including academic certificates and identification proof. Eligible candidates are shortlisted based on merit or entrance examination scores. Selected students are then required to complete document verification and pay the admission fee to confirm their enrollment.
