CSE

Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) is a popular academic discipline combining computer science and computer engineering focusing on core items like programming languages, data structures and algorithms, and software-hardware systems design. You can review comprehensive program info on the Wikipedia Computer Science and Engineering Overview or explore detailed curricula via guides like the Karpagam College of Engineering CSE Guide. 

IT

Information Technology (IT) engineering is a career field focused on designing, building, and maintaining computer systems, networks, and software. Key areas in this field include network administration, software development, and cyber security. 

ECE

Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE) is a core engineering branch that focuses on designing electronic circuits, communication systems, and integrated microchips.

. It bridges hardware and software for technologies like smartphones, 5G networks, and IoT devices. Freshers typically earn an average starting salary of ₹4 to ₹8 LPA.

EEE

EEE engineering stands for Electrical and Electronics Engineering, a core branch focused on power systems, electrical machines, and electronic circuits. It covers power generation, renewable energy, and control systems

Mechanical

Mechanical engineering is a core branch of engineering that designs, analyzes, and manufactures physical systems, machines, and thermal devices. It focuses on the study of objects and systems in motion, combining math, physics, and materials science.

Mechanics and Forces: Studying how structures handle weight, stress, and motion.

Thermodynamics and Fluids: Analyzing heat transfer, energy conversion, and fluid flow.
