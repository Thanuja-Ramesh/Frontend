Zoho

Zoho Corporation is a global technology company founded in 1996 by Sridhar Vembu and Tony Thomas. It provides cloud-based business software, collaboration tools, and office suites

Founding: Started in 1996 as AdventNet before changing its name to Zoho Corporation in 2009.

Headquarters: Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India, with global offices including an American headquarters in Austin, Texas.

Independence: Privately held, bootstrapped, and profitable without venture capital funding.

TCS

Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) is a global leader in IT services, consulting, and business solutions. Founded in 1968, it is headquartered in Mumbai, India, and operates as the flagship subsidiary of the Tata Group

Founding: Established in 1968 by a division of Tata Sons and led early on by F.C. Kohli.

Headquarters: Mumbai, Maharashtra, India.
